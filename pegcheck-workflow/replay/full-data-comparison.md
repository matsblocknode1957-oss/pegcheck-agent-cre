## Follow-up: chronic rule, fixed glitches, variant F+C

### 1. Why the existing chronic rule didn't block alusd

The chronic guard in `rules.ts` fires when `hoursOffPeg > CHRONIC_HOURS` (72).
`hoursOffPeg` is computed as `(now − lastAtPegTs) / 3600000` where `lastAtPegTs` is the
most recent history entry with price within 0.5% of peg.

**The structural problem:** the history window passed to `buildHistoryStats` is
`THREE_DAYS_MS = 72 h` — exactly equal to `CHRONIC_HOURS`. Because `lastAtPegTs`
can only come from within that window, the maximum possible `hoursOffPeg` is 72 h.
The check is strict (`> 72`), so it can **never** be satisfied from replay history.

When the coin has never been at peg inside the 72 h window (`lastAtPegTs === null`),
the fallback is hardcoded to **25 h**. Since 25 < 72, the check fails here too.
alusd has traded persistently at 0.96–0.97 since the dataset begins;
the system cannot distinguish it from a routine 25-hour fresh dip.

The history window is `THREE_DAYS_MS = 72 h`, the same value as `CHRONIC_HOURS = 72`.
`hoursOffPeg` is derived as `(now − lastAtPegTs) / 3600000`. Since `lastAtPegTs`)

The chronic check is `hoursOffPeg > 72` (strict), so it can never be satisfied.
When the coin has never been at peg in the window (`lastAtPegTs === null`), the fallback
is hardcoded to **25 h** — well below the threshold.
alusd has traded at 0.96–0.97 since the dataset begins on 2026-05-22.
Its first buy fires on **candle 1**, with exactly 1 history point(s),
none of which is at peg. The system cannot distinguish this from a fresh 25-hour dip.

### 2. Fixed glitch detector

**Old rule:** `low_median < 0.95` AND close recovers above 0.95 within 3 hours.
**New rule:** `low_median < 0.95` AND the hour **before** AND the hour **after** both closed within 0.5% of peg (≥ 0.995).

The new rule requires the spike to be genuinely isolated — not part of a sustained depeg on either side.
alusd's 'glitches' are not isolated: its surrounding candles are at 0.96–0.97, well below 0.995.
